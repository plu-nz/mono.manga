Privacy

Manga is a PLUNZ app at manga.plu.nz. It works without an account. This page explains what happens to your data.

On your device

Your library, favorites, reading progress, settings, offline chapters, imported files, and generated EPUB files are stored only in your browser (IndexedDB). Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Catalog requests

Searches and chapter lists are requested from MangaDex through a small PLUNZ relay on Cloudflare Workers, because MangaDex does not accept these requests directly from other websites. The relay forwards only the catalog request, does not store it, and does not log your searches. Cloudflare may process your IP address to deliver and protect the service and to apply rate limits. Images are loaded directly from MangaDex servers by your browser. Their privacy policy applies to those requests.

Google (optional)

If you choose to connect a Google account, the app asks for each permission only when a feature needs it:

  • Your email address (openid, email) — to show which account is connected, so you can add it to Amazon's approved sender list.
  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata) — to keep a copy of your library, progress, and settings in a hidden folder that only this app can read. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Send email (gmail.send) — to send the EPUB files you create to your own Send-to-Kindle address from your Gmail account. The app cannot read, list, or delete your email.

All Google requests go directly from your browser to Google. PLUNZ servers never receive your Google tokens, your synced data, or your files. Access tokens are kept in memory and in your browser's session storage until they expire (about an hour). You can disconnect in Settings, delete the synced copy, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Extra chapter sources (authorized accounts only)

Some accounts can unlock extra sources that fill chapters missing from a title. Unlocking sends a Google access token once to the PLUNZ server, which asks Google for the account's email, checks it against a list of authorized accounts, and returns a signed session that stays on your device. The email is not stored. Requests to those sources then go through the PLUNZ server, which relays them without keeping logs of what you read.

AniList (optional)

If you link an AniList account, the app updates the number of chapters read on your AniList list when you finish a chapter, and reads that number to mark earlier chapters as read on this device. Requests go directly from your browser to AniList. The access token AniList issues is stored only on this device, is never synced or sent to PLUNZ servers, and is removed when you disconnect. You can also revoke access in your AniList account settings.

Amazon Kindle

Files you send are delivered by email to the Kindle address you enter, and Amazon processes them under its own terms. Your Kindle address is stored on your device and, if you enable sync, in your Google Drive app data.

Content

PLUNZ does not host or store manga. Titles and chapters come from MangaDex and the scanlation groups credited on each chapter, or from files you import yourself. Please support official releases where available.
